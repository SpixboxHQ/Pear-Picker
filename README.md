# Pear Picker
# Stupid game that sucks

Imagine a game as simple as Cookie Clicker, but delightfully obsessed with pears.

Pear Picker (legally distinct from cookie clicker) is an additive, casual clicker game where every tap grows your pear stash. But here’s the twist: instead of buying standard multipliers, you spend your pears to unlock hilarious, absurd, and 100% real pear facts.

Did you know pears don't ripen on the tree? Now you do—and reading that fact just doubled your Pear-Per-Second harvest.

It combines the addicting loop of incremental gaming with bite-sized trivia collectible cards. Players harvest, unlock weird knowledge, upgrade their orchard, and show off their rare fact collections. It’s lighthearted, wildly simple, and endlessly fresh—the ultimate micro-distraction for the internet.
